# 🎵 Koalamochi: Suno Music Prompts

In Suno, turn on **Instrumental** and use **Custom** mode. Paste the **Style** text into the "Style of Music" box and the **Lyrics** text into the lyrics box. Suno reads the bracket tags as structure.
Download as MP3 and save into `assets/music/` with the exact file name.

Only `bgm.mp3` is required. The other two are optional: the game switches to them during mini-games and bedtime.

---

## 1. Main home theme → `bgm.mp3` (required)

**Title:** Koalamochi Morning

**Style of Music:**
```
kawaii cozy video game background music, instrumental, music box, glockenspiel, ukulele, pizzicato strings, soft marimba, gentle bouncy bass, light brushed percussion, sweet playful happy melody, major key, 105 BPM, warm lo-fi, cute pastel vibe, seamless loop, no vocals
```

**Exclude styles:** `vocals, singing, heavy drums, distortion, dark, sad, epic`

**Lyrics box:**
```
[Instrumental]
[Intro: soft music box melody]
[Verse: ukulele and pizzicato join, bouncy and cute]
[Chorus: glockenspiel melody, happy and playful]
[Verse: marimba variation]
[Chorus]
[Outro: music box, loops back to the beginning]
```

### Tips
- Make 2–4 versions of each track and keep your favorite.
- Suno songs often have a fade-in and fade-out. The game loops the track, so if the loop point bugs you, trim the start and end in **Audacity** (free).
- The 🎵 button at the top of the game turns music on and off. Browsers only start music after your first click or tap.
